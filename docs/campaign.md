# Launch campaign at PoliTO (Level 3, path C)

**Goal (Reply's bar):** one month after launch, first-year Physics I students know Matteo exists and have tried it at least once.

## Name
**Matteo.** The app is named after the classmate you teach: a typical Italian first-year name, easy to say in Italian and English. “Teach Matteo” is the whole idea in two words, and it makes the tool feel like a peer, not a tutor or a bot.

## Tagline
- **"If you can explain it, you know it."**
- Italian: **"Se sai spiegarlo, lo sai."**
- Secondary, for social media: *"Matteo is lost. Can you save him before the exam?"*

## Who we target, and the channel for each

| Audience | Why they care | Channel | Why this channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| First-year Physics I students | Misconceptions show up at the exam, when it's too late | **In-class 2-minute demo** in the first Newton's-laws lecture (with the professor) | Highest trust: the professor endorses it, and everyone is in the room |
| Same | They live on their phones | **Instagram / TikTok** "Save Matteo" reels: a real student fixing one of Matteo's misconceptions in 20 s; QR code to the app | Short, funny and shareable; the misconceptions are inherently relatable |
| Study groups | They already meet before exams | **Posters with QR codes** in libraries and study rooms ("Bring a +1 to your study group") | You meet them exactly where group study happens |
| Student associations and course reps | Word of mouth | **WhatsApp/Telegram course groups** via reps + a "teach Matteo" mini-challenge with a leaderboard of *groups*, not individuals | Peer-to-peer reach, and it rewards collaboration |
| Professors / TAs | They see where the class breaks before the exam | **Email + a 10-minute demo** at the department teaching meeting; a one-page "what you see, what students see, privacy" sheet | Professors decide whether it enters the course |

## Timing (built around the academic year)

| When | What | Why then |
|---|---|---|
| Week 0 (before Newton's laws) | Professor buy-in, posters, reps briefed | Ready before the topic starts |
| Week 1 (Newton's laws lecture) | In-class demo + QR; launch the reels | The first contact happens while the topic is fresh |
| Weeks 2–3 | Weekly "misconception of the week" reel; group challenge in course chats | Keeps it alive; habit before the midterm |
| Week 4 (midterm prep) | "Teach Matteo before the midterm" push; professor shares the anonymized class view in class ("this is where we break; let's fix it") | Peak motivation; it shows students the tool helps them |
| End of month | Measure: share of students who opened the app, tried at least one session, group sessions started | Reply's goal: they know it and have tried it once |

## Inclusion choices
Text-first (no voice needed), Italian and English, works on a phone. Group leaderboards (not individual ones) avoid shaming the students who struggle most.
